## Rules (authoritative)

- Brand spelling is always UniLive’s.
- Each sticker, gift, badge, ring, frame, card, model, effect, and audio item must be an **individual** asset.
- Never render an entire board as one app asset. Boards belong only under `production/unilives-assets/references/` or `unilives_master_source/references/`.
- Every animated asset requires a playable preview before production approval.
- Character previews require front, left, back, and right views.
- Approved character faces, bodies, outfits, horns, crowns, hair, eyes, kingdom colors, skin tone, proportions, and micro-details must not drift.
- Eight kingdoms: Sun, Ice, Forest, Fire, Ocean, Moon, Storm, Celestial.
- Each kingdom requires King, Queen, Prince, Princess, Baby Boy, and Baby Girl.
- Same-kingdom assets must remain visually consistent.
- Lucky and mixed-kingdom characters remain separate variants.
- Rings, frames, and cards animate without voices.
- Stickers require individual action, facial animation, body movement, lip-sync, voice, sound, emotion, clothing, and effects where specified.
- Gifts require true animation and synchronized sound.
- Splash requires approved brand animation, voice, lip-sync, music, and effects.
- Static picture scaling is not accepted as true animation.
- Approved references are specifications — **not** automatically usable runtime assets.
