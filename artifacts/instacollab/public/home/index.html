UniLive’s

UniLive’s is a live social application for going live, chatting with friends, sharing posts and stories, discovering creators, and collaborating in real time.

Who we are: UniLive’s is the live social app on the verified domain uniapplab.com (app: app.uniapplab.com).

What UniLive’s does

  • Go live and join live rooms with chat, gifts, and multi-guest sessions
  • Share photos, reels, and stories with your audience
  • Message friends and discover trending creators
  • Use karaoke, wallet, Game Hub, and creator workspace tools in one place

Why we use Google Sign-In

Optional Google Sign-In lets you create or access your UniLive’s account securely. When you choose Google, we receive basic profile details (such as name, email, and profile photo) to authenticate you. Additional Google Workspace permissions (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and similar) are requested only if you explicitly connect those features in the Admin Panel, and only for providing those in-app tools.
