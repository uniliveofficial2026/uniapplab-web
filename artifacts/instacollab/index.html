UniLive

UniLive is a live social application for going live, chatting with friends, sharing posts and stories, discovering creators, and collaborating in real time.

The purpose of UniLive is to help people connect through live streaming rooms, direct messages, a social feed, karaoke and creator tools, and optional Google sign-in so you can securely access your UniLive account on the web.

  • Go live and join live rooms with chat, gifts, and multi-guest sessions
  • Share photos, reels, and stories with your audience
  • Message friends and discover trending creators
  • Manage your UniLive profile, wallet, and creator workspace in one place
